POSITIONING, SEARCH BOUNDARIES, AND APPLICATIONS
16 October 2026

Search approach
Targeted searches combined AIIB, cofinancing, safeguards, independent
accountability mechanisms, Central Asia, Mongolia, Shokpar, and mutual
reliance; additional searches used Chinese terms for AIIB/joint finance/
accountability and Russian terms for Shokpar/complaints/EBRD. Citation
following connected institutional sources and scholarly literature.
This is a purposive literature review, not an exhaustive systematic review.
No assertion that all languages, unpublished work, or all databases were
searched is made.

Closest prior work and implications

Geary, Goyal, Amoako and Inclusive Development International (2023),
Roadblocks to Accountability: a direct predecessor. It already analyzes
portfolio eligibility, cofinancing exclusions, policy/IAM mismatches,
instrument differences, and problems after referral. Those ideas cannot
be claimed as newly discovered here. The present work adds an explicitly
reproducible regional subset, two-register linkage, an exact lower bound
on loss from published-label compression, and a later Shokpar trace through
2026. It does not claim to be the first portfolio analysis.

Creutz (2023), International Journal of Human Rights: rights protection,
efficiency, and fragmented safeguards. Relevant theoretical precursor;
the present study operationalizes the policy/forum/evidence distinction.

Gu (2023), RECIEL: inheritance and innovation in AIIB's ESF. Important
counterweight to assuming innovation or reliance is inherently harmful.

Ransdell (2019), Asian Journal of International Law: institutional
innovation and cooperation; predates the later complaint trajectory.

Lee and Kim (2025), International Affairs: comparison of the AIIB and
World Bank's development-finance practices and text. Shared policy
language does not settle project-specific forum designation.

Mirza (2018), California Law Review: Southern institutional voice and
local redress are distinct issues. No novelty claimed for this distinction.

Lorenzo (2024), ICLQ; OHCHR (2022), Remedy in Development Finance:
answerability, responsibility, and remedy are distinct. The paper neither
discovers nor collapses these concepts.

Skalamera (2025), International Environmental Agreements; Ponedelnik
(2026), regional just-transition research: frame procedural participation
within regionally specific distributional and institutional relationships.

Existing Shokpar records and coverage
EBRD's reports and registry already disclose the referral and process
events. Accountability Counsel's database and Early Warning System
already index this project. Russian-language news also reports the
controversy. The study analyzes existing documentary facts; it does not
claim first discovery of the complaint or independently verified bird harm.

Scope decisions
1. A general 'cofinancing creates accountability gaps' paper was rejected
   as too close to existing reports and legal scholarship.
2. A national-origin ranking of lenders was rejected: the corpus cannot
   support it, and sponsor, bank, policy and forum have different roles.
3. A causal claim that delegation worsens outcomes was rejected: no
   counterfactual or representative complaint sample is available.
4. The adopted contribution joins a bounded register reconstruction to
   specific evidence circulation after a demonstrably working referral.

Practical landscape
Accountability Counsel's Accountability Toolkit/Console indexes complaints
and stages. The Early Warning System indexes planned/financed operations
and related disclosure. Publish What You Fund's DFI Transparency Index
assesses institutional disclosure. These establish that project discovery,
accountability tracking, and disclosure quality assurance are existing
activities; they do not establish a new paying market for this prototype.

Application hypothesis
A versioned evidence ledger could support community advisers,
sustainability consultants and lender supervision by retaining the exact
policy/forum source, identifying changes, distinguishing process closure
from outcome, and recording requester access to monitoring reports.
Potential paid work would be maintenance, integration, and quality
assurance. The underlying source trail should remain open and inspectable.

No customer interviews, procurement commitments, revenue forecast,
market-size estimate, patent search, or validated commercial benefit is
claimed. Existing public tools are acknowledged rather than represented
as absent. Practical utility remains a deployment hypothesis to test.
