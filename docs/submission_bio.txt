Shivam Gupta is an independent researcher, applied AI engineer, and founder of Siloed, a product and software studio. He studied Computer Science and Design at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi. His professional experience spans software engineering, AI evaluation, product development, and the design of systems that make complex decisions inspectable. His research interests concern the relationship between formal rules, operational systems, and the evidence needed to assess institutional claims. The paper proposed for this workshop develops that interest through infrastructure finance, environmental and social safeguards, and access to accountability mechanisms in Central Asia. It combines public-document analysis with reproducible computational methods while keeping institutional designations distinct from findings about practical access or remedy. He is based in India, with professional experience spanning India and the United Arab Emirates, and will travel from Delhi.
