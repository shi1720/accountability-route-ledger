STUDY RECORD
Date: 16 October 2026
Author: Shivam Gupta

Design
Exploratory public-document research: a census of two bounded institutional
registers, a descriptive regional subset, and a purposively selected process
trace. No preregistration, independent coding panel, causal identification,
or population representativeness is claimed.

Primary quantitative source
AIIB Co-Financed Projects, End-2025, 152 numbered financing entries. The
download filename bears 11 March 2026. Comparator: updated End-2024 version,
136 numbered entries, filename dated 10 January 2025. See sources.json for
the exact bytes' SHA-256 values.

Country rule
Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Mongolia.
No selective inclusion based on technology, complaint, or designation.
Azerbaijan is outside this geographical definition.

Core fields
Published applicable policy and applicable IAM are separate fields. Neither
is inferred from the lead institution, reference institution, sponsor
nationality, or a generic policy label. The IAM field is AIIB's designation;
it is not a census of every possible remedial route.

Checks
PyMuPDF table geometry supplies cells. Poppler independently checks amount,
approval date, reference institution, policy, and IAM for all 288 row
observations. Regional member/amount/policy/IAM fields are additionally
checked against rendered source pages. The latter is within-study checking,
not independent inter-rater validation.

Snapshot identity
The unique tuple is (member, exact full title, approval date, listed
financing). Member/date/amount alone collides for two pairs of operations.
All 136 earlier entries match. Policy and IAM labels remain unchanged.
Differences in other source fields are not claimed absent.

Alternative denominators
1. All regional entries.
2. Exclude sectors beginning CRF-.
3. Published Energy or Transport sector labels.
4. Same labels excluding row 151, the Uzbekistan climate policy operation.
5. Five generation/rehabilitation entries (not renewables-only): rows
   61, 66, 67, 96, and 146.

The register labels row 151 Energy while the current project page labels
it Multi-sector. Both are retained as source-specific observations. This
is why a sector label is not called a verified physical-project boundary.

Amounts and inference
Decimal arithmetic uses the register's nominal USD-million amounts.
Weights are neither outstanding exposure nor risk/harm/compensation.
The census is descriptive; no standard errors or p-values are reported.

Policy-only minimum
Sum over policy groups of (group count minus largest IAM count). The
independent oracle enumerates all choices of an observed mechanism per
policy label. An unobserved label cannot improve either nonnegative count
or amount-weighted error. The calculation is elementary and not claimed
as a new algorithm. It concerns abbreviated published labels.

Process trace
IPAM case 2024/05. Sources: AIIB project description, IPAM assessment and
closure reports, and live case registry retrieved on 16 October 2026.
Allegations, responses, administrative events, and findings are separate.
The four underlying monitoring reports are not part of the inspected
corpus; their quality or ecological conclusions are not independently
adjudicated. Month-only dates remain closed date intervals.

Temporal legal interpretation
The 2026 PPM policy and rules apply to submissions made from 1 January
2026. The Shokpar submission dates to May 2024. No retroactive breach of
the revised policy is asserted. Policy section 11.1's broad coordination
and outcome-reporting provision is kept separate from section 11.2's
additional management report following a noncompliance finding.

Observation cutoff and artifact status
16 October 2026. A future compliance result could change case status.
The manuscript is a research submission, not a peer-reviewed publication.
